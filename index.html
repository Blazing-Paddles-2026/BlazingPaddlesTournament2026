2026 Recap

A day on the courts. A win for the community.

Blazing Paddles 2026 brought players, families, and first responders together for a Round Rock Fire Foundation pickleball tournament built around community, family fun, friendly competition, and support for firefighter families.

🔥

The Cause

Proceeds from the day support Round Rock Fire Foundation programs — firefighter wellness, family assistance, crisis relief, and community outreach for the people who answer the call.

🏓

The Play

Teams competed in men's and women's doubles, mixed doubles, a first-responders division, and a social bracket built for fun.

🎉

The Vibe

Food trucks, kids activities, and a community of players, families, and first responders cheering each other on between games.
